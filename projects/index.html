Selected research

FallacyRAG

2026 · Under review at EACL 2027

Retrieval-augmented fallacy detection and classification in US political debates, with retrieval steered by the support and attack relations between arguments. Improves macro-F1 up to 0.864 for detection and 0.725 for classification over non-retrieval baselines.

Python · RAG · LLMs · Argument mining

RooseBERT

2025–2026 · WI-IAT 2026

A BERT language model for political discourse, pre-trained on 11 GB of English debates and speeches (cased and uncased, from scratch or by continued pre-training). Outperforms general-purpose models on most downstream tasks, from stance detection to NER.

Python · BERT · Pre-training · Hugging Face

PaperModelsCode Read more →

DISPUTool

2025 · ACL 2025 (Demo Track)

A web tool for mining and exploring arguments in US presidential debates (1960-2020), built at Inria and 3IA Côte d'Azur. DISPUTool 3.0 adds a module that detects and classifies fallacious arguments and shows how each argument reads once the fallacy is repaired.

Web demo · Argument mining · Fallacies · LLMs

Political Debates Graph Analysis

2023–2025 · ArgMining@ACL 2025

Code for 'Leveraging Graph Structural Knowledge to Improve Argument Relation Prediction in Political Debates'. Knowledge graph embeddings over the argument graph of US presidential debates predict support, attack and equivalence relations.

Python · PyTorch · PyKEEN · Knowledge graphs

ElecDeb60to20

2023 · Dataset (fork)

Annotated corpus of US presidential debates (1960-2020) with argument components, support and attack relations, and fallacies. A fork of the public dataset repository, which my work on fallacy detection and relation prediction builds on.

Dataset · Argument mining · Fallacies

DebateLens

2026 · iOS app, work in progress

A SwiftUI iOS app that lets journalists read US presidential debates (1960-2020) as annotated argumentation: claims and premises, what supports or attacks them, and the fallacies used. Built on ElecDeb60to20.

Swift · SwiftUI · iOS

ServiceBook

2026 · iOS app

A SwiftUI app that turns a vehicle's owner's manual into a live maintenance checklist, tracking what is overdue, due soon or upcoming by km and months. Claude finds the manual online or reads a PDF and converts it into the schedule; all data stays on the device.
