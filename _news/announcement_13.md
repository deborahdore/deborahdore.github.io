📢 Our paper ["Retrieving Relations, Detecting Fallacies: A RAG Approach To Political Debate Analysis"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27471)
(_Deborah Dore_, Greta Damo, Elena Cabrio, Serena Villata) has been accepted at [EACL 2027](https://2027.eacl.org/)! 🎉
We show that using the support and attack relations between arguments to guide retrieval helps language models detect
and classify fallacies in political debates.
