📢 Our paper "DISPUTool 3.0: Fallacy Detection and Repairing in Argumentative Political Debates" (Pierpaolo Goffredo,
_Deborah Dore_, Elena Cabrio, Serena Villata) has been accepted to [ACL 2025's Demo Track](https://2025.aclweb.org)!
DISPUTool 3.0 is a web-based tool that detects and repairs fallacious arguments in political debates. Built on a
collection of U.S. presidential debates, from 1960 to 2020, DISPUTool goes beyond detection by suggesting revised,
fallacy-free versions of flawed arguments.
